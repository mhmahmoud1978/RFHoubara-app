Poultry Vaccination Tips

Keep vaccines refrigerated (2–8°C)
Maintaining the cold chain preserves vaccine potency.
Use clean equipment
Sterile syringes and droppers prevent contamination.
Vaccinate healthy birds
Only birds free from stress or illness respond well to vaccines.
Follow manufacturer instructions
Mixing, dosage, and administration routes vary by vaccine.
Record each vaccination
Documentation helps track coverage and booster schedules.
Dispose of unused vaccine safely
Follow local guidelines to avoid environmental contamination.
